Hello! I’m Carles.

I am a postdoctoral researcher working on quantum information theory. I am interested in foundational topics like contextuality and entanglement, and quantum information tasks like quantum communication and quantum cryptography. My research is focused in prepare-and-measure and bi-partite Bell scenarios. I also like relaxing problems with semidefinite programming.

I am currently based in the Institut de Ciències Fotòniques (ICFO, Barcelona, Spain) with a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral fellowship.
